What is a Fractional Strategy Executive?

A Fractional Strategy Executive provides high-level strategic guidance—like a CSO or COO—on a part-time or retainer basis. This allows growing businesses in Toronto and beyond to access executive-level expertise without the full-time salary of a C-suite hire.

Do you serve clients outside of the GTA?

Yes. While H-Squared is based in Oakville and primarily serves the Greater Toronto Area (Toronto, Mississauga, Burlington), we work with clients across North America remotely for strategy and AI automation projects.

How can AI automation help my business?

AI automation replaces repetitive manual tasks—like data entry, invoice processing, and customer scheduling—with intelligent software agents. We use tools like n8n and Python to build custom workflows that reduce errors and free up your team for high-value work.

Do you handle website design and build?

Yes. We offer full-stack digital design and infrastructure builds to ensure your business has a modern, high-converting digital presence that can seamlessly support your automated workflows.
